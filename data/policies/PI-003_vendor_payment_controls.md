# PI-003 Vendor Payment Controls

## 1. Bank Detail Changes

A change to vendor bank details must be verified by a call-back to a phone
number already held in the vendor master, never to a number supplied with
the change request. Email-only change requests are treated as suspected
payment diversion. A payment to a vendor whose bank details changed without
a verified call-back within the prior 30 days is a high-severity finding and
must be held pending investigator review (Policy PI-004 Section 3).

## 2. Invoice Amount Controls

Vendor invoices above 25,000 USD require a matching purchase order. Invoices
without a purchase order are held. An invoice that is both above 25,000 USD
and follows an unverified bank change must be escalated to the SIU lead, not
only an investigator.

## 3. Evidence Standard

The case summary must cite the vendor event identifier for the bank change
and the payment identifier for any disbursement already made.
